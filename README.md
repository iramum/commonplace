# Commonplace — Oops, I wrote it again.

A workspace for writers who publish on Medium: notes, ideas, calendar and the life of every published piece in one place.

Portfolio version with synthetic data. The writer (Nora Vale), the publication (The Open Studio), every piece, number, note and quote are invented.
The product, screens and flow come from a personal tool the designer built for her own daily writing work.

- `index.html` — the app (vanilla JS, no build). Open over http: `python3 -m http.server` → `index.html`
- `make_demo_data.py` — generates `data/latest.json` (same shape as the real morning robot)
- Fonts: Newsreader, Inter, Source Serif 4 (free look-alikes of Medium's GT Super, Söhne and content serif)
