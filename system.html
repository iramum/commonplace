Design system

Every colour, type style and component on this page is taken from the working prototype. The page is built from the app's own stylesheet, so nothing here is drawn separately.

Principles

Calm like a reading appWhite, hairlines, black pill buttons. The base is borrowed from Medium, where writers already feel at home.
Yellow means hersHer notes, her published pieces, her badges and the comma. Yellow is never a warning.
Red only where she must actA red number, never a red block. Green only where something grows.
A count before a chartLarge serif numbers answer first. Detail sits one tap deeper.
